Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Michael charge for balloon twisting in Chicago?
Pricing depends on the number of hours, guest count, and location of your event. Contact Michael with your event details for a free, custom quote. Every event is different, so there's no one-size-fits-all price list.
How far in advance should I book Michael?
Two to four weeks ahead is a safe window for most events. Book earlier for weekend dates, holidays, and large festivals or corporate events, when calendars fill up faster.
Does Michael offer balloon twisting in Rogers Park?
Yes, Rogers Park is one of the Chicago neighborhoods Michael regularly serves, from lakefront park gatherings to school and community events. Contact him with your event date and details for availability and a free quote.
What areas of Chicago does Michael serve?
Michael proudly serves Rogers Park and neighborhoods throughout Chicago, including the Chicago Loop, Lincoln Park, Lincoln Square, Edgewater, Wicker Park, Old Irving Park, and Andersonville, plus Evanston and nearby suburbs. Contact us if you're unsure whether your location is covered.
What's included in a balloon twisting booking?
Michael brings all balloons and supplies needed for your event and stays for the full time booked, twisting a custom menu of balloon animals and sculptures for your guests.
What types of events do you work?
Birthday parties, weddings, block parties, school events and carnivals, festivals, corporate events, and fundraisers throughout Chicago and nearby suburbs. See event photos in the gallery.
