Privacy Policy

How Michael Romani Balloon Twisting handles the information you share with us and the information this website collects. Last updated October 7, 2026.

Who we are

Michael Romani Balloon Twisting ("Michael," "we," "us") provides balloon twisting entertainment in Chicago, Illinois and the surrounding area, and runs this website, michaelromaniballoons.com. You can reach us at romanimike@gmail.com or (812) 887-9056.

Information you give us

When you request a quote through the contact form, we collect the details you type in: your name, phone number, email address, event date, event type, estimated guest count, the street address or cross streets of the event, and your message. If you call, text, email, or message Michael on Instagram, we receive whatever you choose to send.

We use this information to reply to you, prepare a quote, schedule and provide the service, and keep a record of the booking. Contact form submissions are delivered to Michael's email through Formspree, a third-party form service.

Information collected automatically

This website uses Google Tag Manager to run Google Analytics and Google Ads measurement tags. These tools may collect information such as the pages you view, the device, browser and operating system you use, your approximate location, how you found the site, and whether you tap the phone, text, or email links, click a quote button, or submit the contact form. When the form is submitted we record that it was submitted and the event type you selected. We do not send what you typed into the form (your name, phone, email or message) to Google Analytics or Google Ads.

Our hosting and content delivery providers (Sevalla and Cloudflare) also receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when you visit, as part of delivering and securing the site.

Cookies and similar technologies

Google Analytics and Google Ads use cookies and similar identifiers to recognize returning visitors and measure whether an ad led to an inquiry. Cloudflare and the embedded Google Map on the Contact page may also set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, though some features may be affected.

  • Opt out of Google Analytics: Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on
  • Control ad personalization: Google Ad Settings

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries, provide quotes, and book and perform events.
  • To understand how people use the website and improve it.
  • To measure whether our advertising brings in inquiries.
  • To protect the site against spam and abuse.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the site and the business, and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Google (Tag Manager, Analytics, Ads, and Maps): how Google uses data from sites that use its services.
  • Formspree (contact form delivery): Formspree privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare and Sevalla (website hosting and delivery): Cloudflare privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety. Links to outside sites, such as Instagram, are governed by those sites' own policies.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and booking messages for as long as we need them to serve you, keep business records, and meet legal requirements. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings in our analytics account.

Photos of events

This website includes photos from events, and some show guests, including children. If you or your child appears in a photo and you would like it removed, email or call us and we will take it down.

Children

This website is meant for parents, guardians, and other adults arranging events. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it by emailing romanimike@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under state law, and we will honor valid requests. You can also opt out of analytics and ad personalization using the links in the cookies section above.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us, including serving this site over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email romanimike@gmail.com or call (812) 887-9056. Michael Romani Balloon Twisting, Chicago, IL.
