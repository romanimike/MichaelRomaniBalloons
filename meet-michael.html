Meet Michael

Meet Michael Romani, Chicago's Balloon Artist

Michael Romani is a professional balloon artist and balloon twisting entertainer in Chicago, IL, with over 9 years of experience. He offers hourly balloon twisting for birthday parties, weddings, block parties, school events and carnivals, festivals, corporate events, and fundraisers throughout the Chicago Loop and surrounding neighborhoods, including Lincoln Park, Lincoln Square, Edgewater, Wicker Park, and Old Irving Park, plus nearby suburbs.

Guests choose from a balloon menu of custom balloon animals and sculptures, and Michael brings that same energy everywhere from living-room birthdays to school carnivals, festivals, and corporate events across Chicago.

Michael's Story

Nine Years of Turning Balloons Into Smiles

For over 9 years, Michael has traveled across Chicago and its neighboring suburbs bringing balloon art to life, one twist at a time. What started as a passion for entertaining kids at birthday parties has grown into a full-service balloon business covering everything from intimate backyard celebrations to large-scale corporate installations.

Every event is different, so Michael treats every balloon menu, arch, and column as a custom job, matching colors, themes, and energy to whatever the occasion calls for. Whether it's a toddler's first birthday, a wedding reception, a school festival, or a company fundraiser, the goal is always the same: leave guests smiling and the space looking incredible.

Based in Chicago, Michael continues to serve the Loop and neighborhoods throughout the city. See the full list of balloon twisting services or browse the photo gallery to see recent work.
